The 3R Disposition Framework

Intelligent Three-Tier Categorization

Our AI model evaluates hardware degradation, component reparability, and economic viability to assign the most eco-friendly pathway.

Tier 1: Restore

Repair

Devices with minor hardware or software issues—such as cracked screens, loose charging sockets, worn batteries, or broken hinges—that can be economically serviced.

Extends hardware lifecycle

Reuse

Recycle
